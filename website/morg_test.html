Pulsars

Pulsars are a type of fast rotating Neutron star that have the distinct charactertic of radiation beams emitted at the poles which is known as the "lighthouse effect". Typically emitting in the radio spectrum, there has been some pulsars that emit in other spectrums like X-ray, and Pulsars like the Crab Pulsar emit across the eletromagnetic spectrum.
